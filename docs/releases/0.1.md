## FM1_AudioBase 0.1

The first release: the M-VAVE FM-1 as a USB audio interface, and the base FM-1 firmware to fork from.

- **USB audio output "FM-1"**: what the computer plays comes out of the FM-1 (48 kHz, 16-bit stereo, resampled to
  the FM-1's own clock).
- **USB audio input "FM-1"**: the computer records what the FM-1 plays, after the volume (48 kHz, 16-bit stereo,
  the codec's own samples).
- **MASTER** is the volume of both: off at the bottom, 0 dB at the top, without zipper noise.
- **The screen** shows both streams, L/R peak meters, the volume in dB, CPU, USB and MIDI activity. PLAY lights
  while the computer plays to the FM-1, REC while it records from it.
- **GLO** toggles the visualizer (MOOD_FM1's: the waveform, fed back on itself).
- No driver on macOS, Windows or Linux (USB Audio Class 1, with USB-MIDI beside it).

Tested on an FM-1. It installs over the official firmware and over other Felucca-platform firmware (SLOOP, ORBIT,
ChoralRoot, GATOR, MOOD_FM1, AMB-1), and back to M-VAVE's V15 from the web installer.
